✦ Intelligent Insights
Equity = allocation + fundamentals

Combines each stock's current allocation (vs. your configured limit under Settings → Equity Allocation Limits) with the fundamentals above. Low allocation alone is never treated as a Buy signal, and high allocation alone is never treated as a Sell signal — click "Why?" on any stock for the full reasoning.

"Import Screener Data" reads an .xlsx with a header row (symbol, book_value, eps, industry_pe, roe, roce, etc. — same column names your Screener export uses) and matches each row to an Equity holding by Symbol. Re-importing replaces the whole Screener dataset. Click any row in the table above to see its full breakdown here.

All data stays in this browser (localStorage) unless you export it or sign in to sync via Google. Nothing is sent to any server except live price lookups and, if signed in, your own Firestore document.
