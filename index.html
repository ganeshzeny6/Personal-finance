Stock Analysis

Fundamentals for your Equity holdings — Stock/Symbol, Sector and LTP come straight from the Equity tab; everything else is joined in from imported Screener data by Symbol. PE, P/B and Yield are always calculated here, never taken pre-computed from the source file.

Stock / Symbol Sector LTP 52W Low 52W High Gain from Low % Drop from High % EPS PE Industry PE Buy Reco Book Value P/B Industry P/B Yield % Div Yield % ROE % ROCE % Debt/Equity Promoter Hold % EPS Gr. 3Y % EPS Gr. 5Y % Sales Gr. 5Y % Qtr Profit Var % Qtr Sales Var %

"Import Screener Data" reads an .xlsx with a header row (symbol, book_value, eps, industry_pe, roe, roce, etc. — same column names your Screener export uses) and matches each row to an Equity holding by Symbol. Re-importing replaces the whole Screener dataset.

All data stays in this browser (localStorage) unless you export it or sign in to sync via Google. Nothing is sent to any server except live price lookups and, if signed in, your own Firestore document.
